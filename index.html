        Sound gallery: my little orchestra of tones

        Every tone I use. All of them are made by your browser as it plays, from simple waves; there are no recorded sound files. Opening tones rise and closing tones fall. Each kind of bracket has its own sound. The deeper a bracket sits inside other brackets, the higher its tone, or, if you choose, the further it moves to the left or right.

              Credits and licences: giving honour where it is due

              The lectures by Professor Pennyfeather were written for Maths by Ear; the Professor is a fictional character. All sounds are synthesised in your browser as they play, so there are no recorded samples and no sound licences to credit. They have been adapted for listening: references, tables and figures removed, long articles shortened, and every formula rewritten as spoken words, with short plain-words explanations added after displayed formulas.
